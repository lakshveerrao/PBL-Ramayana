## The four notes

**The conch is a session signature, not an effect.** The pack: *"this is the session's
opening. One conch phrase with a bell on its attack. **Nowhere else in the arc during
continuous play.**"* M2–M7 are one continuous run and take no signature between them — the
pack closes M1 with *"Hard join into M2: no signature between them."* So the conch is
heard once, at the top, and never again until a new session opens.

**Room tone is the lattice and one lamp**, not just air. HANDOFF §8 scopes the continuous
hall take to "every interior from M2 to M7, across five joins" — M1 is not in that
sentence, because M1's join out is hard rather than continuous. Putting the same tone
under M1 is therefore a choice, and a good one: it is the same hall on the same morning.
It carries one obligation — `graph.roomTone()` says *not reseated across the cut, same
file, same level* — and that rule now has to hold across M1's own fifteen cuts too.

**The water is the only rich sound in the film**, and the pack says why: *"it should be
generous, because the gesture is."* `direction/overrides.json` records why it sits on beat
6 rather than 7 — beat 6 **is** the honouring: a vessel, hands, water poured, the sage's
nod. 01-07 is the king asking.

**No music is binding twice over.** The pack: *"No score at all. M1 is the only film in
the arc with no music, which makes the drone entering in M2 feel like weather changing."*
And `direction/music-decision.json`, which reverses the provider to "generated", keeps the
brief's silences intact — *"no music at all in M1 and M5"*. M1 is silent of music whichever
way the provider question goes.
